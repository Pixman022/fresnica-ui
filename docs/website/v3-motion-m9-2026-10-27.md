## Manual acceptance checklist (Windows Chrome)
1. Open `https://pixman022.github.io/fresnica-ui/fresnica-v3-motion-preview/` in Chrome with hardware acceleration enabled, ideally at 1440×900.
2. Check the Hero fluid surface: green core, translucent texture, slow deformation, smooth 96-tick ring; no black WebGL errors or flashing.
3. Scroll down and back through five pinned chapters; verify copy, asset panel, network stages, and payment path steps change reversibly.
4. Check the light-to-dark footer switch; FRESNICA particles scatter and reassemble without a large layout jump.
5. Toggle OS/Chrome Reduced Motion and keyboard navigation; confirm visible readable fallback and menu/FAQ.
6. Review a 10–20-second screen recording or screenshots from the real GPU browser before marking video-fidelity approval.
