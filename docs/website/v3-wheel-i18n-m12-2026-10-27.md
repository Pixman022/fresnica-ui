# Fresnica V3 M12 — Wheel Snap, EN/ZH, Theme Placeholder, and Alignment

Date: 2026-10-27
Branch: `design/website-vision-motion-concept-2026-10-27`
Scope: The user's four requested refinements only. Original video-framed page, approved Hero WebGL shader, content positioning in M11, and previous M5/M6/M7/M10 scripts remain unchanged.

## Implementation

1. **Desktop wheel threshold / one complete scene at a time:** `m12-experience.js` observes wheel deltas inside the V3 page frame. A cumulative ±115px within 500ms triggers a single smooth scroll to the next/previous chapter runway. Each transition runs ~760–1500ms, followed by a short 260ms momentum suppression. Multiple pinned runways still play their internal M5/M6 state animations while scrolling; progress remains reversible. Ctrl/meta zoom and shift/horizontal gestures are not intercepted. At <=680px and under Reduce Motion, native scrolling remains enabled. No forceful scroll snapping during keyboard or anchor navigation.
2. **EN/简体中文:** Default English; new top-right EN / 中文 control toggles Chinese without reloading. 266 localized text/ARIA labels cover headings, body copy, hero ticker, navigation, dynamic M6 illustrative panels, FAQs, and footer. Restores original English exactly on toggle-back. Brand name, XLM/Stellar identifiers and sample quantities remain unchanged. Long Chinese nav labels collapse on narrow screens rather than overlapping the brand.
3. **Top-right theme placeholder:** New disabled sun/moon-style header control, with clearly marked coming-soon title/aria-label. It does not change the whole-site theme. The existing independently approved footer light/dark demonstration is intentionally retained.
4. **Feature cards and hero ticker:** Responsive feature-track centering distributes 1, 2, or 3 cards symmetrically in the available viewport, while preserving the original scroll-driven horizontal carousel. The hero subtitle/ticker is contained in the same centered 1180px safe area; both edges use subtle fading, ticker remains animated except under Reduced Motion.
